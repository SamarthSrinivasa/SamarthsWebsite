Pitch Bend Synth

Play notes with ASDFGHJKL; and the black keys WETYUOP — same layout as GarageBand's musical typing. Z / X shift octaves. Move your cursor left and right across the strip below to bend pitch, just like the pitch wheel on a physical MIDI keyboard. Click any extra key on the keyboard to play it directly with your mouse.

Click anywhere, or press a key, to start audio.
